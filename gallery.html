Demon Slayer

After a demon massacres his family, kindhearted teenager Tanjiro Kamado discovers that his younger sister, Nezuko Kamado, has survived but transformed into a demon. Determined to avenge his family and find a cure to turn Nezuko human again, Tanjiro joins the secret Demon Slayer Corps

Attack On Titan

The story follows Eren Yeager, his adoptive sister Mikasa Ackerman, and their best friend Armin Arlert. In the year 845, peace ends when the colossal Colossal Titan and the Armored Titan breach Wall Maria, allowing Titans to flood Shiganshina. Eren's mother is devoured, driving him to swear vengeance and enlist in the military alongside Mikasa and Armin.

Jujutsu Kaisen

human negative emotions—such as fear, regret, and hatred—leak out and form dangerous monsters known as Curses. Ordinary people cannot see them, so a secret society of Jujutsu Sorcerers trains to fight them using a special power called Cursed Energy.
